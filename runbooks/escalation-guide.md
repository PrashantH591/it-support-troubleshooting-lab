# IT Support Escalation Guide

## When to Escalate

Escalate an incident when:

- The issue is outside the support scope
- Required permissions are unavailable
- There is a potential security incident
- Multiple users or systems are affected
- The issue requires specialist access
- The issue cannot be resolved within the agreed support process

## Information to Collect

Before escalation, document:

- User/device affected
- Time of incident
- Symptoms
- Troubleshooting performed
- Diagnostic results
- Error messages
- Business impact
- Actions already taken

## Escalation Principle

Provide the next support team with enough information to continue the investigation without repeating basic troubleshooting.
